# Reference attribution

OpenStax College, **2005 Surface Anatomy of the Heart**.

Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2005_Surface_Anatomy_of_the_Heart.jpg

License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/

The original diagram is retained as `openstax-heart.jpg`. `anterior.png` and
`posterior.png` are crops. `anterior-evaluation.png` removes annotation leader pixels
only for silhouette diagnostics; see `annotation-mask-provenance.json`.

The app's procedural 3D model adapts the diagram's external anatomy, color zones
and illustrated detail. Geometry, proportions, material response and detail density
were changed. It is not an OpenStax-authored 3D asset.
